Arkcards Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

Arkcards strictly values user data protection and is committed to handling personal information securely and responsibly.

Information We Collect

To create an account and manage secure user sessions, Arkcards collects standard account credentials, such as your email address or username and password. Passwords are handled through secure authentication services and are not stored by Arkcards in readable form.

If you choose to use marketplace features, we may also process information you provide for listings, profiles, messages, support requests, orders, delivery, or payments. We use this information only to provide the feature you requested, operate the marketplace, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Location and Advertising

Arkcards does not track user location.

Arkcards does not sell personal information or share personal information with third-party advertising companies.

Service Providers

We may use secure service providers for essential functions such as authentication, hosting, payment processing, and order fulfilment. These providers may process only the information needed to perform their services and are not permitted to use it for third-party advertising.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information. We retain information only for as long as needed to provide Arkcards, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Your Choices and Account Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law. For account deletion or privacy inquiries, contact arkana.tarot.market@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Arkcards is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who cannot legally provide that consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Arkcards or applicable legal requirements change. The effective date above will identify the latest version.
