Arkcards Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

Arkcards strictly values user data protection and is committed to handling personal information securely and responsibly.

Information We Collect

To create an account and manage secure user sessions, Arkcards collects standard account credentials, such as your email address or username and password. Passwords are handled through secure authentication services and are not stored by Arkcards in readable form.

If you choose to use marketplace features, we process information you provide for listings, profiles, messages, support requests, orders, delivery, or payments. We use this information to provide the feature you requested, operate the marketplace, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

AI authenticity review is optional. If you request it, we send the first image for that listing to OpenAI to generate an assessment. We do not send listing images for this review unless you opt in. OpenAI processes that image under its service terms. Do not include people or unrelated personal information in verification images.

Location and Advertising

Arkcards does not track user location.

Arkcards does not sell personal information or share personal information with third-party advertising companies.

Service Providers

We use service providers including Supabase for authentication, hosting, and storage; OpenAI for an authenticity assessment only when you request it; and payment providers for checkout. They process information under their service terms. We do not sell personal information or share it with third-party advertising companies.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information. We retain information only for as long as needed to provide Arkcards, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Your Choices and Account Deletion

You can delete your account from Account settings in the app. Deletion removes your profile, listings, chats, support tickets, and uploaded images. Limited transaction or moderation records may be retained where needed for legal, accounting, safety, or dispute-resolution requirements; delivery details are erased. For privacy inquiries, contact arkana.tarot.market@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Arkcards is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who cannot legally provide that consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Arkcards or applicable legal requirements change. The effective date above will identify the latest version.
